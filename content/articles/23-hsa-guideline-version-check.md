*Written by the Neoi - Health Supplements Singapore editorial team - Last updated: 22 August 2026*

An HSA guideline version check confirms a document's title, revision marker, date, live source and scope before you rely on a downloaded Singapore supplement file. Webpage dates and PDF editions are different fields. This guide is educational information, not medical or legal advice.

## Start from the current HSA hub

Begin with HSA's live [health supplements hub](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/health-supplements/), marked "Last updated 5 August 2026" when checked on 22 August 2026. It routes to regulation, claims, notification, reporting, collaboration and classification resources.

The [regulatory overview](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/health-supplements/overview/) was marked "Last updated 15 July 2026" and linked to the Health Supplements Guidelines and safety, quality and labelling Q&A. Use the live pages as navigation records; do not assume an old saved PDF is still the linked edition.

The map does not claim that one source supersedes another. Title, scope and revision identity matter alongside the date.

## Build a seven-field version record

Original element 2, captured 22 August 2026: keep this record beside any HSA supplement PDF used for research, compliance discussion or internal review.

1. **Exact title:** copy the full cover title, not a shortened filename.
2. **Issuing body:** record HSA and the named group or branch when shown.
3. **Document identifier:** capture the guideline code and revision number, such as `GL-CHPB-3-001 Rev. No. 020`, when present.
4. **Visible publication date:** record the PDF month and year or the HTML "Last updated" date.
5. **Live source URL:** save the current official address and access date.
6. **Scope:** write what the source actually covers, such as broad health-supplement requirements, selected safety questions or ASEAN guidance navigation.
7. **Relationship note:** record whether HSA links to the file and whether another source is a summary, FAQ or separate framework.

Do not invent a missing revision number or convert a webpage footer date into a document edition. Record `not shown` when the source does not display a field.

## Resolve version mismatches without guessing

Use a narrow action for each mismatch:

| Observation | Safe interpretation | Next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saved filename matches, but cover revision differs | The files are not the same edition | Reopen the live HSA link and archive the current cover details |
| Webpage update date is newer than PDF month | The page and PDF have different date fields | Check whether the live page still links to that PDF; do not declare it obsolete from dates alone |
| Search result opens an unavailable deep URL | The route is unstable or outdated | Return to the HSA hub or overview and follow its current link |
| Q&A language seems narrower than the main guideline | The documents have different purposes | Read the question in context and keep the main guideline available |
| ASEAN topic appears on HSA's page but detail sits elsewhere | HSA is providing a navigation layer | Follow the named official route and record both sources |

If current official sources appear to conflict materially, record the exact passages and seek clarification through HSA's official channel.

## Know what a version check cannot establish

A current guideline does not approve a product, certify a seller, verify a batch or decide personal suitability. HSA states that health supplements are not subject to its pre-market approval and licensing for importation, manufacture and sale.

The record preserves which official text was consulted and when. Classification, restrictions, claims, labels and individual health questions require separate evidence and, where appropriate, qualified advice.

## FAQ

**Is the newest visible date always the controlling version?** No. An HTML update date, PDF publication month and revision number may describe different source layers. Check title, scope, live linkage and revision identity together.

**Can I rely on a PDF stored on my computer?** Use it only after comparing its cover details with the file currently linked from HSA. A familiar filename alone does not establish version parity.

**Does a current HSA guideline mean a supplement is approved?** No. HSA states that health supplements are not subject to pre-market approval and licensing for importation, manufacture and sale.

**What should I do if official sources seem inconsistent?** Save the exact URLs, dates and passages, avoid a definitive public claim, and seek clarification through HSA's official contact route when the issue is material.
